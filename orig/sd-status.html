— THE FINDING THAT KILLED OUR FINDING
At 4 hours, DBD setups won 68%. At 24 hours, 49%.
Three "headline signals" emerged from the 4-hour forward window: a 32-point win-rate gap between continuation-supply (DBD) and reversal-demand (DBR) setups, a textbook-defying RSI-neutral supply edge, and an Asian-session bias. A single sensitivity check at 24 hours collapsed all three — gaps narrowed to single digits, confidence intervals fell within noise. The 24h confidence intervals are tighter than at 4h, so this isn't a power problem. The 4h "edges" are horizon-specific selection effects.

This is the most important Phase 1.5 result: running one sensitivity check on ourselves prevented a strategy built on artifacts.

How we got here fast

✓
Rule-based foundation first. No ML until we have a labeled dataset and proof of signal.
✓
Proposal-and-verify architecture. Detector surfaces candidates; trader labels them. Trader's bias becomes data, not code.
✓
Causality enforced structurally. Three temporal regions — PAST, FORMATION, FORWARD — make look-ahead leakage impossible by design.
✓
Every decision documented. 10 ADRs. Reversing course is cheap because reasoning is in git.
✓
Findings framed as candidates, not edges. Validation gated on trader input and out-of-sample testing.
